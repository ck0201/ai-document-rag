## 2. Attendance, Leave of Absence & Punctuality Policy (उपस्थिति एवं अवकाश नियम)

### 2.1 Minimum Attendance Requirement
- **CBSE & IB Compliance:** A student must maintain a minimum of **85% aggregate attendance** throughout the academic session to be eligible to appear for final annual and board examinations.
- **Condonation of Shortage:** Shortage of attendance between 75% and 84.9% may only be condoned on verified medical grounds or national-level sports representation, subject to formal sanction by the Principal and the School Management Committee.
- **Attendance Below 75%:** Under no circumstances will a student with less than 75% attendance be allowed to sit for term-end assessments; such students must repeat the academic grade.

### 2.2 Leave of Absence Procedure
1. **Planned Leaves:** For anticipated leaves (family events, religious observances), a written application must be submitted through the Greenwood Parent Portal at least **5 working days in advance** to the Section Coordinator.
2. **Medical Leaves:**
   - For illness lasting up to 2 days, a written diary note signed by the parent is sufficient upon return.
   - For illnesses exceeding 2 consecutive school days, a **Medical Fitness Certificate** and prescription from a registered medical practitioner (MBBS or equivalent) must be submitted within **48 hours** of resuming school.
3. **Continuous Unnotified Absence:**
   - If a student remains absent for **10 consecutive working days** without written notification, their name will be struck off the school register (*De-registration*).
   - Re-admission in such cases requires payment of a re-admission surcharge of **₹5,000 INR**, clearance of all arrears, and explicit approval by the Principal.

### 2.3 Punctuality Slabs & Consequences
- **1st & 2nd Late Arrival in a Term:** Written warning recorded in the student's school diary.
- **3rd Late Arrival:** Mandatory 45-minute after-school detention on the subsequent Friday.
- **5th Late Arrival:** Formal parent conference with the Vice-Principal and forfeiture of one day of privilege leave.
- **More than 5 Late Arrivals:** One-day internal suspension with zero credit for work missed.

## 3. Academic Standards, Evaluation & Promotion Criteria (अकादमिक मानक और मूल्यांकन)

### 3.1 Weightage & Examination Structure
The academic calendar consists of two balanced semesters: **Term 1 (April to September)** and **Term 2 (October to March)**.

| Assessment Component | Period Conducted | Weightage |
|---|---|---|
| Periodic Test 1 (PT-1) | July | 10% |
| Mid-Term Examination (Half-Yearly) | September | 30% |
| Periodic Test 2 (PT-2) | December | 10% |
| Final Annual Examination | February / March | 40% |
| Internal Assessment & Project Portfolio | Year-Round | 10% |

### 3.2 Minimum Passing Thresholds
- **Grades 1 to 8:** Continuous and Comprehensive Evaluation (CCE) with a passing benchmark of 35% overall in each scholastic subject.
- **Grades 9 and 10:** Minimum **40% marks** in each subject, requiring 40% in theory and 40% in internal assessments independently.
- **Grades 11 and 12:** Minimum **40% marks in theory** and **40% marks in practical/viva examinations separately** in science, commerce, and humanities streams.

### 3.3 Academic Honors & Awards
- **Principal’s Gold Scholar Medal:** Awarded to students who secure an aggregate of **95.0% or above** across all terms with 0 disciplinary infractions.
- **High Honors Roll:** Aggregate between 90.0% and 94.9%.
- **Subject Proficiency Award:** Highest marks in an individual subject across the grade level (minimum 92% benchmark).
- **Remedial Mandate:** Students scoring below 45% in any Periodic Test must attend compulsory **Saturday Remedial Clinics** from 09:00 AM to 11:30 AM for four consecutive weeks.

## 4. Code of Conduct & Multi-Tier Disciplinary Matrix (अनुशासन संहिता एवं दंड प्रणाली)

Greenwood High enforces a progressive, corrective disciplinary framework categorized into four distinct tiers based on severity.

### 4.1 Tier 1: Minor Infractions
- **Examples:** Incomplete uniform, unpolished shoes, tardiness to classes, chewing gum, eating in unauthorized areas, incomplete homework.
- **Consequences:** Verbal counseling by Class Teacher, notation in Student Diary, loss of house points (-5 points).

### 4.2 Tier 2: Moderate Infractions
- **Examples:** Repeated Tier 1 violations (3+ times), unauthorized possession of mobile devices or gaming gadgets, bunking classes, using profanity, disrespect toward school support staff.
- **Consequences:** 
  - Mandatory 45-minute detention on Friday afternoons.
  - Written notification issued to parents.
  - Deduction of 15 house points.
  - Disqualification from student leadership elections for one term.

### 4.3 Tier 3: Serious Infractions
- **Examples:** Academic malpractice (cheating in tests, copying assignments, forging signatures), vandalism of school property, verbal intimidation, cyber-bullying, public obscenity.
- **Consequences:**
  - **3 to 5 calendar days of Out-of-School Suspension (OSS)**.
  - Automatic zero score in the test or examination involved in academic dishonesty.
  - In cases of vandalism: Mandatory restitution equal to **100% of repair/replacement cost plus an administrative penalty of ₹2,500 INR**.
  - Loss of 50 house points and loss of any prefectorial/council titles.

### 4.4 Tier 4: Critical Infractions (Zero-Tolerance)
- **Examples:** Physical violence, weapon possession, consumption/possession of tobacco, vapes, narcotics, or alcohol, severe sexual harassment, hate speech, extortion.
- **Consequences:**
  - Immediate summoning of parents within 2 hours.
  - Referral to the School Disciplinary Board.
  - **Permanent Expulsion** or issuance of a Compulsory Transfer Certificate (TC) with conduct remarks logged as "Unsatisfactory".
  - Mandatory filing of an official incident report with local law enforcement if applicable under juvenile statutes.

## 5. Anti-Bullying, Harassment & Cyber Safety Framework (एंटी-रैगिंग एवं साइबर सुरक्षा)

Greenwood High strictly prohibits any form of peer victimization, hazing, or online harassment.

### 5.1 Definition & Scope
Bullying is defined as any unwanted, repeated aggressive behavior involving an observed or perceived power imbalance. This includes:
- **Verbal:** Name-calling, taunting, derogatory comments regarding race, religion, gender, or appearance.
- **Social/Relational:** Intentional exclusion, spreading rumors, public humiliation.
- **Cyber-Bullying:** Posting defamatory messages, memes, or unauthorized photographs of peers or faculty on social media (Instagram, WhatsApp, Discord, etc.).

### 5.2 Anti-Bullying Committee
The school maintains a permanent standing committee comprising:
1. Vice-Principal (Chairperson)
2. Senior School Counselor
3. Head of Physical Education
4. Two elected Parent-Teacher Association (PTA) delegates
5. Head Boy and Head Girl (Ex-officio non-voting members)

### 5.3 Reporting & Resolution Timelines
- **Anonymous Reporting:** Confidential Drop Boxes positioned outside the Infirmary and the Wellness Center, or via secure email: `antibully@greenwood.edu`.
- **Preliminary Investigation:** Commences within **24 hours** of filing a complaint.
- **Formal Hearing:** Concluded within **72 hours** with both student parties and their parents.
- **Whistleblower Protection:** Any act of retaliation against a student reporting bullying triggers immediate **Tier 3 disciplinary action**.

## 7. Digital Devices, Electronics & Artificial Intelligence (इलेक्ट्रॉनिक उपकरण और AI नीति)

### 7.1 Smartphone & Smartwatch Policy
- **Absolute Campus Prohibition:** Possession of personal cellular smartphones, smartwatches with SIM/cellular capability, and mobile gaming consoles is forbidden during school operational hours (07:15 AM to 03:30 PM).
- **Commuter Exemption:** Students commuting via public transit who carry a phone must switch off the device upon passing through the campus security gate and deposit it in the **Designated Security Lockers at Gate 1**.
- **Confiscation Penalties:**
  - **1st Infraction:** Device confiscated for **14 calendar days**. Returned solely to a parent/legal guardian in person after signing an undertaking.
  - **2nd Infraction:** Device confiscated for the **remainder of the academic term** and a penalty of **₹1,500 INR** charged to the student's incidental account.
  - **3rd Infraction:** Device confiscated until the end of the academic year; student barred from bringing any digital equipment permanently.

### 7.2 Permitted Technology
- **Laptops & Tablets (Grades 9 to 12 only):** Permitted exclusively for designated BYOD (Bring Your Own Device) classroom periods with written authorization from the IT Department.
- **Calculators:** Non-programmable scientific calculators (e.g., Casio fx-82MS or equivalent) are authorized **only for Grades 11 and 12** during Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry examinations. Graphing and programmable calculators are prohibited.

### 7.3 Academic Integrity & Generative AI Policy
- Using generative artificial intelligence platforms (such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Copilot) to generate full essays, assignments, or lab reports without explicit faculty consent is classified as **Academic Plagiarism (Tier 3 Infraction)**.
- AI tools may be utilized strictly as assistive research aids provided all prompts, outputs, and contributions are explicitly cited in an **AI Attribution Appendix** submitted alongside the coursework.

## 8. School Transport, Bus Safety & Commute Regulations (स्कूल बस और परिवहन नियम)

The school operates an extensive fleet of 48 air-conditioned buses covering designated arterial routes across Bengaluru and neighboring suburbs.

### 8.1 Fleet Safety Standards
- Every vehicle is fitted with certified speed governors limiting maximum velocity to **40 km/h**.
- GPS telematics with live parent tracking via the **Greenwood Commute Mobile App**.
- Dual HD CCTV cameras capturing passenger seating and driver cabin with 30-day cloud recording retention.
- An accredited female transport attendant (*Aaya*) and a licensed driver accompany every route.

### 8.2 Bus Protocol for Students
1. Students must arrive at their assigned pickup point at least **7 minutes prior** to the scheduled boarding time. The bus will not wait beyond **2 minutes** past schedule.
2. Boarding and deboarding are permitted **only at registered, assigned bus stops**.
3. **Change of Route/Stop:**
   - Temporary one-day drop change: Written consent submitted by parent to the Transport Supervisor at least **24 hours prior** (`transport@greenwood.edu`).
   - Permanent stop alteration: Requires formal application with **7 working days notice**, subject to seat availability.
4. **Behavior on Board:** Standing while the vehicle is in motion, leaning out of windows, eating snacks, or unruly shouting is strictly prohibited.
5. **Bus Suspension Penalties:**
   - 1st Infraction: Written caution.
   - 2nd Infraction: Suspension of bus travel privileges for **5 consecutive school days** (parents must arrange commute).
   - 3rd Infraction: Complete revocation of transport allocation for the semester with **no fee refund**.

The Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Media Resource Center houses over 35,000 volumes, international periodicals, and digital research databases.

### 9.1 Borrowing Privileges & Quotas
- **Grades 1 to 5:** 1 book for a maximum duration of **7 calendar days**.

### 9.2 Overdue Charges & Book Replacement
- **Late Return Surcharge:** **₹15 INR per book per day** for the first 7 overdue days; increases to **₹30 INR per day** thereafter.

  - The borrower must replace the book with an identical, brand-new hardcover/paperback copy of the same or newer edition.
  - If out of print, the borrower must reimburse the full market replacement cost plus a **25% administrative acquisition charge**.
- **Clearance Certificate:** All borrowed materials must be returned, and pending dues settled, before the issuance of Annual Admit Cards.

## 10. Science & Technology Laboratories Safety Rules (विज्ञान प्रयोगशाला नियम)

### 10.1 Laboratory PPE & Protocols
- Students entering Chemistry, Biology, Physics, or Robotics labs must wear a knee-length **white cotton laboratory coat** and **safety goggles**. Open-toed footwear is forbidden.
- Long hair must be securely tied back; loose scarves or ties must be tucked in.
- Food, beverages, chewing gum, and water bottles are completely banned inside laboratory premises.

### 10.2 Chemical Handling & Breakage Penalties
- Pipetting by mouth is strictly banned; mechanical pipette pumps must be used at all times.
- All concentrated acid reactions must be performed under the supervised **Fume Hood**.
- **Apparatus Breakage Fee:** Any glassware, electrical multimeter, or microscope damaged due to student recklessness will be billed at actual replacement cost plus a flat **₹300 INR laboratory service fee**.

## 12. Health, Wellness & Campus Infirmary Protocols (स्वास्थ्य एवं चिकित्सा केंद्र)

### 12.1 Infirmary Operations
- The campus hosts a modern 6-bed Infirmary staffed by **two full-time Registered Nurses (RN)** and a visiting consultant pediatrician from 07:30 AM to 04:30 PM.
- Emergency tie-up: **Manipal Hospital, Whitefield (3.2 km from campus)**. The school maintains a dedicated, on-campus air-conditioned ambulance equipped with oxygen and automated external defibrillators (AED).

### 12.2 Medication Administration
- No student is permitted to self-administer medication in classrooms, hostels, or lockers.
- All prescription medications must be handed over to the Head Nurse in original packaging, accompanied by:
  1. A formal doctor’s prescription dated within the last 30 days.
  2. A signed **Parent Medical Administration Consent Form**.
  3. Clearly documented dosage, administration timing, and known allergy notes.

### 12.3 Communicable Disease Quarantine Periods
To prevent campus-wide outbreaks, students recovering from infectious illnesses must observe the mandatory home quarantine period before returning:

| Illness | Mandatory Quarantine Duration | Return Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Chickenpox (Varicella) | **14 calendar days** from appearance of first vesicle | All scabs completely dried and crusted; Doctor's certificate |
| Measles / Rubella | **7 days** after complete disappearance of rash | Pediatrician Clearance |
| Conjunctivitis | Until eyes are completely clear of discharge | Infirmary Nurse inspection |
| Mumps | **9 days** after onset of parotid swelling | Medical Certificate |
| Dengue / Typhoid | Minimum **7 days** after full defervescence (fever abatement) | Complete blood count (CBC) recovery clearance |
| COVID-19 / Influenza | **5 days** of home isolation + 24 hours fever-free without antipyretics | Negative rapid antigen or fitness letter |

## 16. Emergency Procedures & Crisis Response Protocols (आपदा प्रबंधन और आपातकालीन नियम)

### 16.1 Drills & Frequency
- Fire and earthquake evacuation drills are conducted **once every academic quarter** without prior notice to the student body.
- Target evacuation time for all campus blocks to designated muster areas is **under 3 minutes and 30 seconds**.

### 16.2 Audible Siren Signals
1. **Continuous High-Pitched 60-Second Siren:** Immediate Fire Evacuation. All occupants must exit via designated stairwells (elevators automatically grounded).
2. **Intermittent 3-Second Staccato Pulses:** Earthquake / Structural Hazard. Immediate *Drop, Cover, and Hold* protocol under heavy desks until siren stops.
3. **Three Short Chimes Repeated Twice:** Campus Lockdown (Severe External Hazard). Classroom doors locked, blinds drawn, all occupants silent away from windows.
4. **Single Continuous Low-Tone Chime:** All-Clear signal issued solely by the Incident Commander / Principal.

### 16.3 Designated Safe Muster Zones
- **Muster Point Alpha (North):** Primary Athletic Turf Field (Grades 1 to 5).
- **Muster Point Bravo (South):** Senior Football Arena & Track Oval (Grades 6 to 12).
- **Muster Point Charlie (East):** Administrative Courtyard (Staff, Visitors, and Pre-Primary).
